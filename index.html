Why Sanad

A broker, not an insurer.

We place cover with insurers. We do not pay claims ourselves. The useful part of that is practical: a second pair of eyes on the cover, the wording, and the first call after a loss.

01

Help comparing suitable cover

We ask what you need to protect, then approach insurers for cover that fits. You are not limited to one company’s product.

02

Clearer policy terms

Before you decide, we explain what is included, what is left out, and which conditions matter.

03

Guidance when a claim happens

We help you notify the insurer and follow the file. The insurer decides whether a claim is paid.
